## Chosen metadata

- **Name (30/30):** Phone Cleaner: Clean Up Photos. It covers "phone cleaner", "cleaner", "clean up", "clean phone" and "clean up phone", and reads as a plain description of the app.
- **Subtitle (30/30):** Duplicate Photo & Video Finder. It adds "duplicate", "duplicate cleaner", "duplicate photo finder" and "video cleaner".
- **Keywords (96/100):** storage,delete,remove,similar,screenshots,space,free,clear,iphone,swipe,cleanup,pictures,manager. With the title words this covers "phone storage", "clear storage", "clean up storage", "storage cleaner", "storage manager", "delete duplicate photos" and "remove duplicate photos".

# Rework: Shotfile (after the 4.3 rejection)

The cleaner name above was dropped: App Review rejected it under 4.3 as indistinguishable from existing phone cleaners. The app was refocused on screenshots.

DataForSEO `keywords_for_app` (US, English) for the leading screenshot organizers, Captr (6738889624), Clutter (6799858360), Screenshot Manager - Organizer (1548953848) and Tote (6757465631), shows a small niche. The relevant terms are "screenshot tool" (360), "ipad screenshot" (352), "pictures screenshots folder" (180), "copy screenshot" (145), "delete screenshots" (67) and "screenshot software" (60).

Many recent clone names were already taken (ShotSift, ShotKeep, several "Sift" apps), so the name leads with a distinctive brand:

- **Name (30/30):** Shotfile: Screenshot Organizer
- **Subtitle (30/30):** Search & Clear Old Screenshots
- **Keywords (96/100):** delete,copy,text,find,receipts,tickets,codes,live photo,stills,duplicate,storage,folder,pictures
