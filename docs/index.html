Phone Cleaner: Clean Up Photos

Free up photo and video storage on your iPhone. Phone Cleaner finds duplicates, similar shots, screenshots and large videos, and lets you swipe through your library to keep or delete.

  • Duplicates: copies of the same picture, grouped with the best one marked to keep.
  • Similar photos: bursts and near-identical shots taken moments apart.
  • Screenshots and large videos: the biggest space users in your library, sorted for you.
  • Swipe to clean: left to delete, right to keep, with undo.
  • Storage freed: see how much space each cleanup clears.
  • Private: photos are analyzed on your iPhone with Apple's Vision framework and never uploaded.
