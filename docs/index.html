Shotfile: Screenshot Organizer

Find any screenshot by the words in it. Shotfile reads your screenshots on your iPhone, sorts them by kind, and points out the ones that are out of date.

  • Search by text: type a word you remember (a booking code, a street, a price) and find the screenshot it's in.
  • Sorted for you: codes and logins, tickets and passes, receipts, chats, directions, posts and notes.
  • Ready to clear: one-time codes older than two days and tickets whose dates have passed, grouped so you can delete them together.
  • Copy and share text from any screenshot.
  • Live Photos to stills: keep the picture, its date, location and albums, and drop the motion that takes up space.
  • Duplicate photos, similar shots and large videos, plus a swipe review to keep or delete one at a time.
  • Private: text recognition runs on your iPhone with Apple's Vision framework. Nothing is uploaded.
