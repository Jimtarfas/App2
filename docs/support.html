Frequently asked questions

How does search work?

The first time you open Shotfile it reads the text in each screenshot on your iPhone. After that, type any word you remember into the search field to find the screenshots that contain it. New screenshots are read the next time you open the app.

How are screenshots sorted?

Shotfile looks at the words in each screenshot: a verification code goes under Codes & Logins, a boarding pass under Tickets & Passes, an order confirmation under Receipts & Orders, and so on. Screenshots with little or no text go under Pictures.

What does "out of date" mean?

Two things: one-time codes (verification and sign-in codes) that are more than two days old, and tickets or passes whose dates have all passed. Nothing is deleted automatically. You can open each one, keep it, or delete the ones you choose.

What happens when I turn a Live Photo into a still?

Shotfile saves the picture (with any edits) as a normal photo with the same date, location, favorite flag and albums, then deletes the Live Photo after you confirm. The original goes to Recently Deleted for 30 days, so you can get it back.

I deleted screenshots but my storage didn't change. Why?

iOS keeps deleted photos in the Recently Deleted album for 30 days. To get the space back right away, open the Photos app, go to Recently Deleted, and tap Delete All.

Some screenshots aren't found.

If you gave Shotfile access to selected photos only, it can only read those. Tap Choose More Photos on the home screen or in Settings. Screenshots stored only in iCloud, without a copy on your iPhone, are also skipped, because nothing is downloaded during a scan.

What does the free plan include?

Reading, sorting, search, copying text and Swipe Review are free. The free plan deletes or converts up to 20 items a day. Shotfile Premium removes that limit.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the iOS Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Shotfile. You can also tap Manage Subscription in the app's Settings. If you cancel during the free trial, you won't be charged.

I reinstalled the app. How do I get Premium back?

Open the Premium screen or Settings and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you subscribed with.
