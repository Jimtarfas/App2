Frequently asked questions

How does Phone Cleaner find duplicates and similar photos?

It uses Apple's Vision framework on your iPhone to compare how photos look. Copies of the same picture are grouped under Duplicates. Near-identical shots taken within a few minutes of each other are grouped under Similar Photos. In each group the photo marked Best (a favorite, or the highest resolution) is kept by default.

I deleted photos but my storage didn't change. Why?

iOS moves deleted photos and videos to the Recently Deleted album for 30 days so you can change your mind. To get the space back right away, open the Photos app, go to Recently Deleted, and tap Delete All.

Can I get a deleted photo back?

Yes, for 30 days. Open the Photos app, go to Recently Deleted, select the photo and tap Recover.

Some photos aren't scanned.

If you gave Phone Cleaner access to selected photos only, it can only check those. Tap Choose More Photos on the home screen or in Settings. Photos stored only in iCloud, without a preview on your iPhone, are also skipped, because nothing is downloaded during a scan.

What does the free plan include?

Scanning, Swipe to Clean and all the lists are free. The free plan deletes up to 20 items a day. Phone Cleaner Premium removes that limit.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the iOS Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Phone Cleaner. You can also tap Manage Subscription in the app's Settings. If you cancel during the free trial, you won't be charged.

I reinstalled the app. How do I get Premium back?

Open the Premium screen or Settings and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

Does Phone Cleaner speed up my iPhone?

No. Phone Cleaner only helps you free up storage used by photos and videos. It doesn't change your iPhone's performance, battery or other apps.
