Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

Phone Cleaner: Clean Up Photos ("Phone Cleaner", "the app") helps you find and delete photos and videos you no longer need. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: we don't collect any of it.

Information we collect

None. Phone Cleaner has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. The app does not send your photos, videos, usage data or device identifiers to us or to anyone else.

Your photos and videos

With your permission, Phone Cleaner reads your photo library to find duplicates, similar shots, screenshots and large videos. The analysis runs entirely on your iPhone using Apple's Vision framework. Photos and videos are never uploaded.

To make later scans faster, the app keeps a small on-device cache of image fingerprints (numbers that describe how a photo looks, not the photo itself). It stays in the app's private storage and is removed when you delete the app.

Phone Cleaner only deletes items you select, and iOS asks you to confirm every deletion. Deleted items go to the Recently Deleted album in the Photos app, where you can recover them for 30 days.

Purchases

Phone Cleaner Premium is an auto-renewable subscription sold through the App Store. Payment is handled by Apple. We never receive your name, email address or payment details. The app checks your subscription status with the App Store on your device.

Permissions

  • Photos: used to scan your library and to delete the items you choose. You can give access to all photos or only selected ones, and change this at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Children

Phone Cleaner does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
