Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

Shotfile: Screenshot Organizer ("Shotfile", "the app") reads, sorts and searches the screenshots in your photo library, and helps you remove the ones you no longer need. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: we don't collect any of it.

Information we collect

None. Shotfile has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. The app does not send your screenshots, photos, the text in them, your searches, usage data or device identifiers to us or to anyone else.

Your screenshots and photos

With your permission, Shotfile reads the text in your screenshots using Apple's Vision framework, entirely on your iPhone. It uses that text to sort screenshots into categories, find dates (for example on a ticket), and let you search. It also compares how your photos look, on your iPhone, to find duplicates and similar shots.

To make later scans faster, the app keeps the recognized text and small image fingerprints (numbers that describe how a photo looks, not the photo itself) in its private storage on your device. They are removed when you delete the app.

Shotfile only deletes or converts the items you choose, and iOS asks you to confirm every deletion. Deleted items, including Live Photos you convert to stills, go to the Recently Deleted album in the Photos app, where you can recover them for 30 days.

Text you copy or share from a screenshot goes only where you choose to paste or share it.

Purchases

Shotfile Premium is an auto-renewable subscription sold through the App Store. Payment is handled by Apple. We never receive your name, email address or payment details. The app checks your subscription status with the App Store on your device.

Permissions

  • Photos: used to read and sort your screenshots, find duplicate photos and Live Photos, and delete or convert the items you choose. You can give access to all photos or only selected ones, and change this at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Children

Shotfile does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
